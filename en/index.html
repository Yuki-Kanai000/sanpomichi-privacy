Privacy Policy — Sanpomichi

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Sanpomichi (“the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

1. Data we use and why

The app handles the following data only on your device (and in your own iCloud). It is never sent to the developer’s servers or to any third party.

DataPurpose
Location (GPS)Creating walking routes, live tracking during a walk, and showing your walked path
Motion & Fitness (steps)Counting steps in real time during a walk
Step count from HealthShowing today’s total steps (read-only; the app never writes to Health)
Weight (entered by you)Estimating calories burned
Walk records and Favorite RoutesShowing history, statistics, goals, and the Coverage Map; reopening saved routes

2. Where data is stored

  • The data above is stored only on your device.
  • iCloud sync: Walk records and Favorite Routes are stored in your own iCloud account (in the app’s own area of Apple’s iCloud Drive) so they carry over when you change devices. The developer cannot access this data. If you turn iCloud off, no syncing takes place.
  • Widget: Today’s steps and your latest walk are shared only between the app and its widget (through an App Group).
  • Apple Watch: Elapsed time, steps, remaining distance, and route coordinates are exchanged only with your paired Apple Watch (through WatchConnectivity).
  • The app has no user accounts or sign-in, and does not collect data linked to you.
  • Data read from Health is never sent or stored outside your device.

3. Third parties and tracking

  • The app uses no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track your behavior.
  • We do not share or sell your data to anyone.

4. Background location

During a walk, the app keeps tracking your location with GPS in the background to record an accurate path. iOS shows its standard location indicator (the blue bar). Tracking stops when you end the walk.

5. Sharing your walk

You can share a walk (route map, distance, time, steps, and calories) as an image. The image is created on your device and is passed on only when you choose to send it through the iOS share sheet, and only to the destination you pick. It is never uploaded to the developer’s servers.

6. Permissions

The app uses the following permissions. You can change each of them at any time in the Settings app.

  • Location (While Using / Always)
  • Motion & Fitness
  • Health (reading step count)

7. Deleting your data

You can delete records from History inside the app. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. Data stored in iCloud can be removed in Settings → your Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

8. Disclaimer

This app is not a medical device. Steps, calories, distance, and other values shown are estimates and are not guaranteed to be accurate. Please consult a professional for health and exercise decisions.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated without prior notice. If there is an important change, we will tell you in the app or on the App Store.
